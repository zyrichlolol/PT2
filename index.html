𑣲⋆PROBLEM SOLVING AND LOGIC

In the industry, coding is simply the tool; problem solving is the core job.

꩜Decomposing Problems:The ability to break down a massive, complex, abstract challenge into small, manageable and logical steps is vital.

꩜Understanding the Problem:Taking time to understand exactly what needs to be solved always results in more useful code than rushing straight to typing.

𑣲⋆SYSTEMS THINKING

꩜Understanding the stack:You must be able to visualize how data flows between the front-end, the back-end, and external databases.

꩜Debugging and Testing:Writing code is only half the battle. Knowing how to use debugging tools and qrite reliable unit tests saves systems from crashing.

𑣲⋆AI LITERACY AND PROMPTING

꩜Complex Prompts:The skill lies in framing highly complex system structures, which AI still struggles to design on its own.

⤷ ゛COMPUTER ERGONOMICS ˎˊ˗

Sit with your back straight and supported by the chair.
Keep your feet flat on the floor or on a foot rest.
Position the monitor at eye level and about an arm's length away.
Keep your wrists straight while typing.
Keep the keyboard and mouse close to avoid reaching.

➤Neck pain - It results from poor posture or spending too much time looking down on the screen
➤Back pain - It is a result of bad sitting posture or lack of proper seating arrangement
➤Wrist and hand ijuries - This arises from repetitive typing and mouse clicking, hence resulting in conditions like Carpal tunnel syndrome.
➤Bad posture - It results in stiff muscles, body pains and spinal aches.
➤Obesity and poor blood circulation - Due to spending many hours seated with minimal physical excersice.
➤Stress and fatigue - Spending long hours on the computer without rest makes one experience mental and physical tiredness.
➤Insomnia - Late evening viewing of screen reduces melatonin hence causing difficulty sleeping.
